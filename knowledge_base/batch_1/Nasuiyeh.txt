--- 📖 SUMMARY ---
Nasuiyeh (Persian: نسوئيه, also Romanized as Nasū’īyeh) is a village in Dar Agah Rural District, in the Central District of Hajjiabad County, Hormozgan Province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 146, in 36 families.
